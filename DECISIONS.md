# Decisions

**83 entries, D1–D82.** Every numbered decision made while
building Thresher, with the reasoning that produced it.

## What these are

These were **written as the project ran, not reconstructed afterwards.** Each
entry was recorded in the session that made the decision, while the reasoning was
still live and before anyone knew whether it would turn out to be right. That is
the only reason the wrong ones are still here.

**A decision without its *why* is a changelog.** The reasoning is the artifact —
what was considered, what it cost, what was given up, and in several cases what
had to be undone later.

**The reversals are the most interesting entries in this file.** They are kept
deliberately, in place, with their original reasoning intact rather than edited
into hindsight:

| Entry | What it overturned |
| --- | --- |
| **D40** | A correction to its own prose: the decision stood, but the usage order it documented (verify → store) was backwards for a new account |
| **D47** | Amends **D43** — Appearance became a fifth Settings row |
| **D49** | Amends **D34** — the refresh cadence the original model got wrong |
| **D57** | Reversed the default list order after tier-first ordering put *zero* messages from the last 14 days on page one |
| **D59** | Overturned the id-list bulk contract — "everything matching this filter" was not expressible in it at all |
| **D63** | Reversed the write-back gate after a standing preference caused a write nobody asked for |
| **D67** | Narrowed **D66**'s always-on backend to the app's lifetime |
| **D71** | A flat correction: macOS does **not** mask an app icon, which the previous decision had assumed |

## Reading notes

- **The numbering has gaps.** D39 was never allocated — a skipped number, not a lost
  decision. **D58 was allocated twice**, caught in audit, and the second
  claimant renumbered to D59; the commit messages on that work retain the stale
  number by design. That was a numbering collision, not a reversal — D58 and
  D59 are unrelated decisions.
- **Two rows are amendments** rather than new allocations — `D52 (built)` and
  `D53 (built)` record what their own decision looked like once implemented.
- Names have been replaced with roles and the author's employer with
  `example.com`. Where an entry's meaning depends on the identity behind it, the
  structure was preserved rather than flattened: **D56** turns on two mailboxes
  sharing one personal domain, so both map onto a single reserved domain
  (`example.net`) and the `*@example.net` group pattern still visibly fires on
  the second mailbox, which is the entire point of the entry.
- `P1`–`P5` refer to the principles in
  [`constitution.md`](constitution.md); `OI` numbers are open items and `E`
  numbers are errors/lessons, both tracked in the project log that is not
  published.

| D38 | **Rule-effect invariant enforced post-merge + a schema CHECK backstop** | Chosen Session 10 (api-gap Phase B).
